Colour options

Five colour schemes on the Hero Split layout. The first two are the palette already in use, split into its light and dark forms; the other three are new. Every scheme is pinned — it looks the same on your screen as on the screen you show it on, rather than following each viewer's system theme.

  • JLT Cool - LightThe current palette, pinned to light. JLT neutrals, navy panels.
  • JLT Cool - DarkThe current palette, pinned to dark. Cool slates derived from the navy.
  • SignalThe brand CTA gradient promoted to the panels. Loudest and most salesy.
  • Steel BlueTheir link blue as structure, orange kept for the money. Most technical.
  • Pastel HeroThe soft cream-to-mint wash from jltmobile.com's own hero. Friendliest.
  • JLT BannerBuilt from the /rugged-computers hero banner: its cream-to-mint wash, its warm off-white, its navy, and its CTA gradient on the buttons.
