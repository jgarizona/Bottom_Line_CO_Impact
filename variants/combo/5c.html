Bottom-Line Impact

What does the wrong computer cost your bottom line?

The core business of your warehouse is moving product out and to your bottom line. When a vehicle computer fails, the warehouse keeps running, but it pays for the disruption: workload shifts to other workers, overtime creeps in, IT and Maintenance gets pulled off its normal work.

10 forklifts. 1 computer fails. Output doesn't fall 10% — other people and equipment absorb it. The real hit is often just 2–3%. Applied to a warehouse's full daily output, that's still real money.

Time period: annual failure rate is the share of the fleet expected to fail within a rolling 12‑month period. Repair/restore time is the number of calendar business days from failure to a working unit back on the vehicle — there is no spare-swap shortcut in this model; every failure is assumed to run the full restore time.
